# Independent visual review

The reviewer saw only task/contract and anonymized 1280×900 / 390×844 screenshots. X = Skill arm B; Y = baseline A. The mapping was revealed after the verdict. This is one AI review, not a human usability study.

**Preference: X.** Desktop keeps the list and editor together; mobile preserves list-before-editor, making selection discoverable. Typography differentiates titles and fields. Y is more compact on desktop, but its mobile screenshot places the arrangement list outside the initial viewport.

**Weaknesses in preferred X:** tall header, unused desktop space, incorrect “HOUSEHOLD PLANNING” audience label, redundant selected-event metadata, and editing/save below the initial mobile viewport.

**Weaknesses in Y:** detached selection/editor, weak selected-state relationship and repeated introductory text; core navigation is not visible in the mobile capture.

Static screenshots cannot establish save, persistence, draft or keyboard behavior. The browser observations cover those separately. The preferred output still needed repair.
